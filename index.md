# Stop! — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 10, 2026_

This policy explains what information the Stop! mobile app ("the app", "we") currently handles and why. It describes the app as it works today; as the app evolves, this policy may change (see [Changes to this policy](#changes-to-this-policy)).

## Information stored on your device

The current version of the app saves your game progress (such as your best level) only on your device. It is not sent to any server operated by us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded ads (watched to retry a level), served by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

- the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
- interactions with ads (views and taps),
- diagnostic and crash information related to ad delivery.

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do not receive or store this information ourselves.

## Your choices and consent

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before ads are personalized, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: _Settings → Google → Ads_; iOS: _Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking_).
- If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

In the current version of the app, your game progress does not leave your device. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security practices.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features, online services (such as accounts, cloud saves or leaderboards), analytics, or new third-party providers, or when the law changes. Such changes may involve collecting, storing or processing additional information about you or your use of the app.

When we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we will also give notice in the app or on this page before it takes effect. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before collecting new types of personal information, and the app will request any new device permissions through your device's standard permission prompts, which you can accept or decline.

Your continued use of the app after an updated policy takes effect means the updated policy applies to you.
